### 🔬 Research
- **Melanoma immunotherapy & the gut microbiome:** sole-author paper accepted to **IEEE BIBM 2026**. Showed that lab-specific batch effects, not treatment response, dominate public metagenomic datasets. Built [`batch_detector`](https://github.com/ames943/batch_detector) to flag them.
- **1840s state debt defaults:** economics research ([EconomicsProj](https://github.com/ames943/EconomicsProj)) mentored by **Thomas Sargent** and **George Hall**: *"Does Default Stay Local? City and State Credit in the Wake of America's 1840s No-Bailout Commitment"*
- **Wildfire dispatch with reinforcement learning:** [InfernoTactics](https://github.com/ames943/InfernoTactics), UCLA COSMOS, under Prof. Tad Blair
- **AI data center cooling:** AP Research simulation comparing hybrid vs. air-only cooling across climates (in progress)
